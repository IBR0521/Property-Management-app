Terms

The software, and the subscription.

These terms cover use of the software at ownerslease.com by a property company and its staff. They are the agreement for the subscription. They are not a lease, a brokerage agreement, or an agreement to hold money.

The price

The price is $2 a month for each door, in US dollars. A door is a unit the company has added. Ten doors are $20 a month. One door is $2. There is no extra fee on tenant rent, and collecting more rent does not change the bill.

The trial

A new company can work for 30 days before a plan is required. When that trial ends with no plan, the company can still be opened and read. Nothing is deleted. Writing starts again when a plan is chosen.

Renewal and cancellation

A plan renews every month until the company cancels it. Cancellation starts from Your plan inside the app, and it is completed on the billing page. The plan runs until the next bill. A bill that fails does not delete the company.

Rent

Tenant rent is charged with the Stripe or PayPal key the company pastes. The money settles in that processor account. This software records the payment. It does not receive, hold, or pay out client money, and it is not a bank, an escrow, or a money transmitter.

The company's own work

The company is responsible for its leases, notices, books, and for the keys it pastes. The software does not score applicants. An emergency report is saved even when a text message cannot be sent. Spend over an owner's limit waits for a person. Paying an owner waits for a person.
