Common questions

What sellers and buyers typically ask

Do I need to repair the property before selling?

No. We assess properties in their current condition and factor the state of the home into the offer. You do not need to renovate or stage before we talk.

Which Columbus neighborhoods do you cover?

We work throughout Columbus, OH and the broader Franklin County area. If you are unsure whether your address falls in our range, call us and we will confirm right away.

How quickly can you close?

Timeline depends on the transaction type, but we work with buyers and title companies to keep things moving. If speed matters to you, say so up front and we will build the schedule around it.

Can you help me find a property to buy, not just sell one?

Yes. We carry active listings and work with buyers looking for homes in Columbus. Browse current listings on this site or call us to describe what you need.
