Plain terms

What the price does, and does not, buy

Thirty days, then a plan

A new company works for 30 days. Choose a plan from Your plan before that ends. If it ends with no plan, the company can still be read. Nothing is deleted.

It renews until you cancel

$2 a month for each door, billed until you cancel. Cancel it from Your plan. That opens the billing page, and the plan runs until the next bill.

No cut of the rent

Tenant rent is charged on the company's Stripe or PayPal and settles there. This software never holds client money, and it adds no fee of its own on that charge.

Questions

Before you start

Does this software hold the rent?

No. The pay button creates the charge with the company's own Stripe or PayPal key. The money settles in that processor account. The software records the payment on the lease.

What does a company pay?

$2 a month for each door. Ten doors are $20. One door is $2. The first 30 days are a trial. After that it renews each month until you cancel it from Your plan. It then runs until the next bill.

Can a tenant pay by bank?

Yes, when the company has pasted a Stripe key. Card and bank run on Stripe's page. PayPal is a second button, and it needs the company's own PayPal key. Card numbers are typed on the processor's page.

Do you score applicants?

No. A person reads the application. The software does not rank people. Confirming the company email, pasting the processor key, importing a bank file, approving spend over an owner's line, and paying owners still need a person.
